## Privacy Policy for Binary Calc

**Last updated:** May 13, 2026

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the **Binary Calc** mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience.

### 2. Data Collection

* **Personal Data:** **Binary Calc** does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as name, email, or phone number).
* **Device Information:** Our application does not access your contacts, camera, location, or any other sensitive device sensors.
* **Usage Data:** No data regarding your calculations or app usage patterns is transmitted to external servers.

### 3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking tools that collect user data.

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Since **Binary Calc** does not collect any personal information, it is safe for use by individuals of all ages, including children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

### 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your professional email.
